This is the first post on argha.dev.

I've been writing code for 9+ years now — across C#, Java, TypeScript, Python, and more. Worked on enterprise telecom platforms, authorization services, deployment tools, and everything in between. But I never had a place to write about what I'm building.

That changes now.

## What to expect here

I'll be writing about:

- **Things I build** — side projects like [Globo](https://github.com/golden-screen) (a streaming platform) and [OnlineExam](https://github.com/ArghaRay00/OnlineExam) (recently modernized from .NET 4.5 to .NET 9)
- **Technical decisions** — why I chose grouped APIs over a single aggregate endpoint, why Carter over controllers, why Astro over Next.js for this blog
- **Tools and workflows** — what works, what doesn't, what I'd do differently
- **Lessons from enterprise** — patterns from building systems at scale that apply to personal projects

## About this site

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build), deployed on [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com). Zero JavaScript by default. Markdown files for blog posts. Push to GitHub, auto-deploys in seconds.

The domain `argha.dev` is my little corner of the internet. Everything I build will live here.

See you around.
