I've been meaning to start writing for a while. Not because I have some big insight the world needs — but because writing helps me think, and I keep running into problems worth remembering.

## Why this exists

I spend my days building enterprise services and my evenings on side projects. Along the way, I make mistakes that cost me hours — a caching header that 404'd forever, a UUID with a typo that passed compilation but failed at runtime, a React hook that captured a stale closure.

Most of these aren't interesting in isolation. But the pattern of how I found them, what I tried first, and what actually worked — that's the useful part. At least for future me.

So this is a place to write that down. Architecture decisions, debugging sessions, things I learned by doing them wrong first.

## What to expect

Posts about backend systems, deployment, infrastructure, and whatever I'm building at the time. No fixed schedule. I'll write when I have something worth your time.
