I'm Argha Ray, a full-stack engineer with 9+ years of experience building enterprise systems, internal tools, and side projects. Currently at **Motorola Solutions** in Bangalore, working on authorization services, deployment automation, and telecom platforms.

I write Java, C#, TypeScript, Python, and Bash professionally. On weekends I build things in NestJS, React, Go, and whatever else catches my attention.
